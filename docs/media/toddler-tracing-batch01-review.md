# Toddler tracing and Batch 01 review

- Tracing: the former stroke hit radius was only 10–20 dp even for the youngest band. Ages 2–3 now default to a 56 dp wide visual trail with rounded ends, gentler coverage and travel thresholds, and no numeric accuracy rating. Older profiles retain the narrower dotted practice mode. The guide button lets a confident three-year-old move to dots or an older child choose support. A wide trail still requires real movement in sequence. Assisted taps cannot become synthetic independent stroke samples. A first completion without a measured score no longer defaults to 85%.
- Home: the Wonder Reels spotlight now gives the title and play icon separate measured space. Real World has separate rows for Cards/Reels and the four categories. Reel details use a single title overlay and the controls can scroll.
- Batch 01: compared all 36 declared payloads with their supplied SHA-256 hashes. Ten supplied WebP cards are packaged unchanged. Five SVG shape designs are represented as Android vectors. The supplied thumbnail and animal lesson are connected to the native catalog. The MP4 was reduced to 960×540, retaining the embedded AAC narration and the 25-second timeline. English subtitles follow playback time. The matching separate MP3 is not played alongside the embedded audio.
- Existing IDs are preserved for matching cards. Mango, Car, five shapes, and the pilot lesson are new entries. Parent Corner distinguishes catalog entries from entries that actually have bundled pictures or video.
- Other attached media packs and individual movement clips are separate from the named Batch 01 integration. Do not label their assets as integrated until matched and exercised.
- Regression scope includes garden, piano, story, word building, Cards/Reels, and timed caption playback. A pass is not proof of playtesting every activity or validating physical speaker output.
